# 🎙️ Anti-Mental Block & Interview Playbook
← Back to [[Home]]

## 1. How to Not Get Mentally Blocked When Speaking in Front
Mental blocks happen when your brain tries to **think of the idea** and **edit the grammar/delivery** at the exact same millisecond, overflowing your "working memory."
* **Speak in "3-Bullet Frameworks" (Not Scripts):** Never memorize word-for-word paragraphs. If you forget one word, the whole script crashes. Instead, only hold a 3-part skeleton in your head:
  * **Point $\rightarrow$ Story/Example $\rightarrow$ Takeaway**
* **Externalize Your RAM:** Put your transitions on slides or index cards. When you look at a slide, it should trigger the next thought automatically.
* **The "Pause & Breathe" Reset:** When your mind goes blank, **stop talking and take a slow 2-second breath**. To you, 2 seconds feels like an eternity; to the audience, it looks like confident, deliberate pacing.
* **Drop Your Gaze to One Friendly Face:** Scanning a crowd triggers fight-or-flight. Lock eyes with *one* person nodding, finish one sentence to them, then move to another person.

## 2. How to Handle Interviews or Sudden Questions Without Freezing
When an interviewer asks a tough question, buy your brain 5 seconds of processing time before committing to an answer:
* **Step 1: The Bridge (Buy 3–5 Seconds):**
  * *"That's a great question—let me break that down into two parts: how I handled the architecture, and how we tested it."*
  * *"Good question. If we're looking at this from a scalability standpoint..."*
* **Step 2: Use the STAR / PREP Formula:**
  * **PREP (For Technical/Opinion Questions):** **P**oint $\rightarrow$ **R**eason $\rightarrow$ **E**xample $\rightarrow$ **P**oint.
  * **STAR (For Behavioral Questions):** **S**ituation (1 sentence) $\rightarrow$ **T**ask (1 sentence) $\rightarrow$ **A**ction (What *you* built/solved) $\rightarrow$ **R**esult (Metrics/outcome).
* **Step 3: If You Genuinely Don't Know the Answer:**
  * Never guess or freeze. State what you *do* know, and explain **how you would figure it out**: *"I haven't deployed that specific service in production yet, but in a similar setup using Docker and Supabase, I solved that bottleneck by [X]. Here is how I'd debug/research it..."*

## 3. What Can I Do Right Now to Improve? (Daily 5-Minute Drills)
- [ ] **The 60-Second "Rubber Duck" Drill:** Once a day, open your phone voice recorder or OBS, pick one feature you coded today (or one educational video you watched), and explain it out loud in 60 seconds with **zero cuts**.
- [ ] **Kill Filler Words with Silence:** Every time you feel the urge to say *"uhm"* or *"ah"*, force your mouth closed for 1 second instead.
- [ ] **Mock Q&A with Obsidian Copilot / Voice AI:** Feed your Resume and Git projects into AI and say: *"Ask me 5 tough technical interview questions one by one and critique my answers."*
