## Why Duolingo Fails
Duolingo optimizes for **streak retention and gamification**, not fluency. Translating isolated, unnatural sentences (*"The bear drinks milk"*) trains pattern recognition for multiple-choice quizzes, not real-time audio comprehension or speaking recall.

## The Most Efficient Method (Comprehensible Input + High-Frequency Output)
Language acquisition works fastest when you combine **4 layers based on how your brain actually wires vocabulary**:

1. **Phase 1: The Top 1,000 Words + "Sentence Mining" (Weeks 1–4)**
   * In almost every language, the top 1,000 most frequent words account for **~80% of daily conversation**.
   * Use **Anki (Spaced Repetition)** with **full conversational phrases/sentences** (never isolated single words) paired with native audio.
2. **Phase 2: Comprehensible Input ("Elementary Style" $\rightarrow$ Native Media)**
   * Start with **"Elementary / Graded" video & audio** where you understand ~70–80% of the context visually (e.g., kids' shows, graded YouTube vlogs with target-language subtitles).
   * **Reading + Audio Together:** Read short stories or transcripts *while* listening to native audio so your brain links spelling to real pronunciation.
   * **Music:** Great for **pronunciation, rhythm, and slang retention**, though less efficient for grammar since lyrics bend sentence rules. Use songs you love to memorize chorus phrases effortlessly.
3. **Phase 3: Conversational "Islands" & AI Roleplay**
   * Build "Conversational Islands"—pre-mastered 2-minute scripts about *yourself* (your job as a dev, your hobbies, where you live) so you always have safe ground in a conversation.
   * Use Voice AI to practice zero-pressure daily conversations before speaking with native speakers.
